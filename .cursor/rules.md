# Cursor Rules

Before working on a Nook task, read `.cortex/AGENTS.md` and
`.cortex/knowledge-graph.md`.

Follow the root router to exactly one Gizmo or team entry point. Load only the
task-relevant authorities selected through that context.

Do not duplicate Cortex policy in Cursor rules. Cortex owns semantic team,
capability, context, security, architecture, workflow, validation, and delivery
authority.

Follow the root team worker contract and the routed operational workflow.
